A. GENERAL CIRCUITS OVER THE FULL BINARY BASIS (B_2), EXPLICIT FUNCTIONS

Claim A1. Find, Golovnev, Hirsch and Kulikov prove a (3+1/86)n - o(n) size lower bound for circuits over the full binary basis computing an explicit affine disperser for sublinear dimension, the first improvement over Blum's 3n - o(n) (1984). Conference version FOCS 2016; ECCC TR15-166 (17 Oct 2015); Revision 2 (19 Aug 2022) is retitled "Improving 3n Circuit Complexity Lower Bounds".
Source: ECCC TR15-166. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2015/166/
Quote: "We consider Boolean circuits over the full binary basis." and "We prove a (3+1/86)n-o(n) lower bound on the size of such a circuit" (function: "an affine disperser for sublinear dimension").
Status: verified (fetched). FOCS 2016 venue verified via the Li-Yang abstract (A2), which cites "Find et al. (FOCS, 2016)".

Claim A2. Li and Yang prove a 3.1n - o(n) lower bound for affine dispersers (functions constructible in P), the current record for explicit functions over B_2. ECCC TR21-023 (21 Feb 2021); conference version STOC 2022.
Source: Jiatu Li, Tianqi Yang, "3.1n - o(n) Circuit Lower Bounds for Explicit Functions", ECCC TR21-023. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2021/023/
Quote: "we prove a stronger lower bound 3.1n - o(n) for affine dispersers" ; "a 3n-o(n) explicit lower bound by Blum (TCS, 1984) was unbeaten for over 30 years." ; "affine dispersers, a class of functions known to be constructible in P".
Status: verified (fetched) for the bound and function. That the bound is for B_2 circuits, and the STOC 2022 venue, are unverified (search snippet only).

Claim A3. Before Li-Yang the best general-circuit bounds had been "around 3n for several decades", and gate elimination is inherently limited to below 5n.
Source: Golovnev, Kulikov, Williams, "Circuit Depth Reductions", arXiv:1811.04828. https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.04828
Quote: "The best known size lower bounds against unrestricted circuits have remained around 3n for several decades." ; "gate elimination, is inherently limited to proving lower bounds of less than 5n."
Status: verified (fetched).

Claim A4 (2026). Carmosino, Dang and Jackman (Feb 2026) formalize gate elimination as a convergent term-graph rewriting system and give the first constructive (refuter-based) lower bound via gate elimination, re-proving Schnorr's 3(n-1) bound for XOR in the DeMorgan basis; no new numerical bound for B_2 is claimed.
Source: arXiv:2602.17942. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17942
Quote: "generalizing Schnorr's classical result that the XOR function requires 3(n - 1) gates to compute in the DeMorgan basis." ; "until this work, no constructive lower bound had been proved via gate elimination."
Status: verified (fetched).

OPEN (A). No explicit function is known to require more than 3.1n - o(n) gates over B_2 (lacker.io problem page, August 2026: unverified, search snippet only). 2026 snippet: Ran Raz, ECCC TR26-008, natural proofs for super-linear lower bounds for linear functions (snippet only; verified separately by S0, see LANDSCAPE.md).

B. DE MORGAN FORMULAS AND THE KRW CONJECTURE

Claim B1. Hastad (SIAM J. Comput. 1998) proved the shrinkage exponent of de Morgan formulas is 2, giving n^{3-o(1)} for Andreev's function; still the best for explicit functions.
Source: Semantic Scholar record DOI:10.1137/S0097539794261556 (title/year only); Golovnev, Kulikov, Williams arXiv:1811.04828.
Quote: "For DeMorgan formulas, the best known size lower bounds have been stuck at around n^{3-o(1)} for decades."
Status: verified (fetched) for the title and the state of the art; attribution to Hastad verified via Tal's abstract (B2).

Claim B2. Tal (FOCS 2014): Andreev's function requires de Morgan formulas of size Omega(n^3/(log^2 n log log n)).
Source: Avishay Tal, "Shrinkage of de Morgan Formulae by Spectral Techniques", ECCC TR14-048. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2014/048/
Quote: "has formula size at least Omega(n^3/(log^2 n log log n))" ; "improves the work of Hastad [SIAM J. C., 1998] by removing logarithmic factors".
Status: verified (fetched). FOCS 2014 venue: unverified (memory).

Claim B3. KRW conjecture (Karchmer, Raz, Wigderson 1995): validity implies P not in NC^1; de Rezende, Meir, Nordstrom, Pitassi, Robere prove it for every monotone inner function whose depth is lower-bounded by lifting, and in a semi-monotone setting; general conjecture open.
Source: ECCC TR20-099. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2020/099/
Quote: "They showed that the validity of this conjecture would imply that" P not in NC^1 ; "it is an important challenge to prove the KRW conjecture for a wider range of inner functions." ; "We prove it for every monotone inner function g whose depth complexity can be lower bounded".
Status: verified (fetched). Venues unverified (search snippet only).

Claim B4. Meir (FOCS 2023): a KRW-like theorem for strong composition; the remaining obstacle is showing ordinary composition behaves like a direct sum.
Source: ECCC TR23-078. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2023/078/
Quote: "We prove a variant of the KRW conjecture for strong composition, thus overcoming the above second obstacle." ; "This result demonstrates that the first obstacle above is the crucial barrier toward resolving the KRW conjecture."
Status: verified (fetched). FOCS 2023 venue: unverified (search snippet only).

OPEN (B). Super-cubic de Morgan formula lower bounds; the KRW conjecture; P vs NC^1.

C. AC0 AND AC0[p]

Claim C1. Hastad (STOC 1986) used the switching lemma to prove tight exponential lower bounds for parity against depth-k AC0, improving Furst-Saxe-Sipser and Ajtai; the bound is exp(Omega(n^{1/(d-1)})).
Source: Wikipedia, "Switching lemma". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switching_lemma
Quote: "It was first introduced by Johan Hastad to prove that AC0 Boolean circuits of depth k require size" [formula omitted] "to compute the parity function on n bits." ; "improving on the prior super-polynomial lower bounds of Merrick Furst, James Saxe and Michael Sipser" "and independently Miklos Ajtai."
Status: verified (secondary: Wikipedia); explicit formula unverified (search snippet only). [S0 note: the formula exp(Omega_d(n^{1/(d-1)})) is stated in the abstract of arXiv 2609.24188, read by S0.]

Claim C2. Rossman (STOC 2008): constant-depth circuits for k-clique need size omega(n^{k/4}).
Source: Crossref record of DOI 10.1145/1374376.1374480 (no abstract).
Status: title/venue/year verified (fetched); the bound is unverified (search snippet only).

Claim C3. Razborov (1987) and Smolensky (1987): MOD_m not in AC0[p] for prime p unless m is a power of p.
Source: Wikipedia, "ACC0". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACC0
Quote: "More generally, the function MOD_m cannot be computed in AC0[p] for prime p unless m is a power of p."
Status: verified (secondary: Wikipedia).

Claim C4 (OPEN: composite moduli). No lower bounds against AC0[m] for composite m are known for NP or any class below NQP; before Williams 2011 it was not known whether EXP^NP had depth-3 polynomial-size MOD6 circuits; whether ACC0 computes majority is open.
Source: Crossref record (with abstract) of Williams, JACM 61(1), 2014, https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1145/2559903 ; Wikipedia "ACC0".
Quote: "Previously, it was not known whether EXP^NP had depth-3 polynomial-size circuits made out of only MOD6 gates." ; "It is conjectured that ACC0 is unable to compute the majority function of its inputs."
Status: verified (fetched) for the first; verified (secondary: Wikipedia) for the second. NP vs AC0[6] open in 2026: inference from the NQP frontier.

Barrier notes (C): a June 2026 arXiv paper (arXiv:2606.12631) claims an unconditional natural-proofs barrier for AC0-natural proofs: snippet only here; verified separately by S0 (see LANDSCAPE.md).

D. ACC0 AND THE ALGORITHMIC METHOD

Claim D1. Williams (CCC 2011; JACM 61(1), 2014): NEXP does not have polynomial-size nonuniform ACC circuits, and E^NP does not have 2^{n^{o(1)}}-size ACC circuits, via faster-than-brute-force ACC satisfiability algorithms.
Source: Crossref record (with abstract) https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1145/2559903
Quote: NEXP "does not have nonuniform ACC circuits of polynomial size" ; E^NP "doesn't have nonuniform ACC circuits of 2" [exponent garbled].
Status: verified (fetched). CCC 2011 venue verified (secondary: Wikipedia).

Claim D2. Murray and Williams (STOC 2018): easy witness lemma for NP and NQP; for every fixed k, NQP does not have n^{log^k n}-size ACC o THR circuits.
Source: ECCC TR17-188. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2017/188/
Quote: "for every fixed k, NQP does not have n^{log^k n}-size ACC o THR circuits." ; "This significantly extends the Easy Witness Lemma of Impagliazzo, Kabanets, and Wigderson".
Status: verified (fetched). STOC 2018 venue verified (secondary: Wikipedia).

Claim D3. Chen, Lyu and Williams (FOCS 2020): almost-everywhere lower bounds: f in E^NP not approximable by 2^{n^{epsilon}}-size ACC0 circuits for all large n; rigid matrices in P^NP for all but finitely many n.
Source: ECCC TR20-150. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2020/150/
Quote: "We show there is a function f in E^NP such that for all sufficiently large input lengths n" ; "We construct rigid matrices in P^NP for all but finitely many inputs".
Status: verified (fetched). FOCS 2020 venue: unverified (search snippet only).

Claim D4. Chen and Ren (STOC 2020): for all constants a, NQP cannot be (1/2 + 2^{-log^a n})-approximated by 2^{log^a n}-size ACC0 o THR circuits.
Source: ECCC TR20-010. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2020/010/
Quote: "We prove that for all constants a, NQP = NTIME[n^{polylog(n)}] cannot be (1/2 + 2^{-log^a n})-approximated" "by 2^{log^a n}-size ACC^0 of THR circuits".
Status: verified (fetched). STOC 2020 venue: unverified (search snippet only).

Claim D5 (barriers). The JACM abstract says nothing about barriers. Search-only: the 2024 Godel Prize citation says the result overcame relativization, natural proofs and algebrization [S0 verified this citation directly, see LANDSCAPE.md]; Vyas and Williams (ECCC TR24-113) give oracle constructions [verified by S0]; Chen, Hu, Ren, "New Algebrization Barriers to Circuit Lower Bounds" (arXiv:2511.14038, ITCS 2026) [verified by S0].
Status: unverified (search snippet only) in this sheet; see S0 notes.

Claim D6 (2022 to 2026). No result found that moves the ACC0 frontier below NQP. Dev Nag, ECCC TR26-167 (Sept 2026): a.e. near-cubic wire lower bound for E^NP against SYM o THR and THR o THR: unverified (search snippet only).

E. TC0 / THRESHOLD CIRCUITS

Claim E1. Kane and Williams (STOC 2016): first super-linear gate and super-quadratic wire lower bounds for depth-two linear threshold circuits with arbitrary weights: Andreev's function cannot be computed on a (1/2+epsilon) fraction of inputs by depth-two LTF circuits with o(epsilon^3 n^{3/2}/log^3 n) gates or o(epsilon^3 n^{5/2}/log^{7/2} n) wires.
Source: arXiv:1511.07860. https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.07860
Quote: "We prove the first super-linear gate lower bounds and the first super-quadratic wire lower bounds" "for depth-two linear threshold circuits with arbitrary weights".
Status: verified (fetched). STOC 2016 venue: unverified (memory).

Claim E2 (OPEN: TC0). Best known TC0 lower bounds are only slightly super-linear; Chen and Tell (STOC 2019) show n^{1+c^{-d}} wire bounds for small enough c would give TC0 != NC1; known only for c about 2.41.
Source: ECCC TR18-199. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2018/199/
Quote: "The best-known lower bounds for the circuit class TC^0 are only slightly super-linear." ; "Lower bounds of the form n^{1+c^{-d}} against these functions are already known" "for a fixed c approx 2.41 that is too large to yield new lower bounds via our results."
Status: verified (fetched).

F. MONOTONE CIRCUITS

Claim F1. Razborov (1985): super-polynomial monotone lower bounds for clique and n^{Omega(log n)} for perfect matching; Alon and Boppana (Combinatorica 1987) exponential for clique.
Source: ECCC TR25-102 (quote); Semantic Scholar record DOI:10.1007/BF02579196 (title/venue/year).
Quote: "This improves on the n^{Omega(log n)} lower bound of Razborov (1985)."
Status: Razborov matching bound verified (fetched via TR25-102); Alon-Boppana numerical bound unverified (search snippet only).

Claim F2. Cavalar, Kumar and Rossman (Algorithmica 2022): exp(n^{1/2-o(1)}) monotone circuit lower bound for an explicit function (Harnik-Raz function), improving exp(n^{1/3-o(1)}) of Andreev and Harnik-Raz; n^{Omega(k)} for k-CLIQUE up to k = n^{1/3-o(1)}.
Source: arXiv:2012.03883. https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03883
Quote: "to obtain an exp(n^{1/2-o(1)}) lower bound on the monotone circuit size" ; "improving the previous best known exp(n^{1/3-o(1)}) due to Andreev and Harnik and Raz."
Status: verified (fetched).

Claim F3. Pitassi and Robere (STOC 2017): 2^{alpha N} lower bounds for an explicit monotone function in NP in monotone formulas, switching networks, span programs and comparator circuits (not general monotone circuits).
Source: ECCC TR16-188. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2016/188/
Quote: "we prove size lower bounds of 2^{alpha N} for computing an explicit monotone function in NP" ; "monotone formulas, monotone switching networks, monotone span programs, and monotone comparator circuits".
Status: verified (fetched). STOC 2017 venue: unverified (memory).

Claim F5 (2025). Cavalar, Goos, Riazanov, Sofronova, Sokolov: perfect matching requires monotone circuits of size 2^{n^{Omega(1)}}.
Source: ECCC TR25-102 (22 July 2025; Rev. 1 6 Nov 2025); arXiv:2507.16105. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2025/102/
Quote: "requires monotone circuits of size 2^{n^{Omega(1)}}" ; "This improves on the n^{Omega(log n)} lower bound of Razborov (1985)." ; "Our proof uses the standard approximation method together with a new sunflower lemma for matchings."
Status: verified (fetched). Rao, ECCC TR26-129 (bipartite matching): unverified (search snippet only).

Claim F6. Tardos (Combinatorica 1988): the monotone vs non-monotone gap is exponential.
Source: Semantic Scholar record DOI:10.1007/BF02122563 (title/venue/year).
Status: title/venue/year verified (fetched); content unverified (search snippet only).

G. NEAR-MAXIMUM CIRCUIT LOWER BOUNDS AND RANGE AVOIDANCE

Claim G1. Chen, Hirahara, Ren (STOC 2024): S_2E/1 contains a language of circuit complexity at least 2^n/n; same for Sigma_2E, (Sigma_2E cap Pi_2E)/1, ZPE^NP/1; previously only half-exponential bounds; corollary of a zero-error pseudodeterministic algorithm for range avoidance infinitely often.
Source: ECCC TR23-144. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2023/144/
Quote: "with circuit complexity at least 2^n/n" ; "Previously, only "half-exponential" circuit lower bounds for these complexity classes were known".
Status: verified (fetched). STOC 2024 venue: unverified (search snippet only).

Claim G2. Zeyong Li (STOC 2024): single-valued FS_2P algorithm for Avoid for all input lengths; S_2E not in i.o.-SIZE[2^n/n].
Source: arXiv:2310.17762; ECCC TR23-156. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17762
Quote: "single-valued FS_2P algorithm for Avoid that works for all input size n."
Status: verified (fetched).

Claim G3. Korten (FOCS 2021): constructing a hard truth table is complete for APEPP under P^NP reductions.
Source: arXiv:2106.00875. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00875
Quote: "constructing a truth table of high circuit complexity is complete for APEPP under P^NP reductions".
Status: verified (fetched).

Claim G4. Ren, Santhanam, Wang (FOCS 2022): Avoid is APEPP-complete; circuit lower bounds for E^NP are equivalent to circuit-analysis algorithms with E^NP preprocessing.
Source: ECCC TR22-048. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2022/048/
Quote: "circuit lower bounds for E^NP are equivalent to circuit-analysis algorithms with E^NP preprocessing."
Status: verified (fetched).

Claim G5 (2026). Ren and Williams (July 2026): near-maximum 2^n/n lower bound for E^{prMA}/1.
Source: ECCC TR26-118. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/118/
Quote: "We prove a near-maximum (2^n/n) circuit lower bound for the complexity class" "corresponding to exponential time with access to a promise-MA oracle and one bit of advice."
Status: verified (fetched). FOCS 2026 acceptance: unverified (search snippet only).

OPEN (G). Super-polynomial circuit lower bounds for E^NP, NEXP, NP remain open (inference from the frontier).

H. DEPTH-3 CIRCUITS

Claim H1. Paturi, Pudlak, Zane (1997/1999): depth-3 circuits for parity need size Omega(n^{1/4} 2^{sqrt n}); best depth-3 explicit bound remains 2^{Theta(sqrt n)}.
Status: unverified (search snippet only; CJTCS page not fetched).

Claim H2. Valiant's depth-3 reduction (log-depth linear-size circuits as OR of 2^{epsilon n} n^{delta}-CNFs).
Source: Golovnev, Kulikov, Williams arXiv:1811.04828.
Quote: "the classical depth-3 reduction results of Valiant, which show that logarithmic-depth circuits of linear size can be computed by an OR of" 2^{epsilon n} n^{delta}-CNFs.
Status: verified (fetched); Valiant 1977 original not fetched.

Claim H3. Goos, Guan, Mosnoi (2023/2024): (9/5)^n-type lower bound for depth-3 bottom-fan-in-2 circuits for inner product; Gurumukhani, Kleber, Paturi, Rosin, Talebanfard (2026): matching upper bound.
Source: arXiv:2601.04446. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04446
Quote: inner product "can be computed by depth-3 bottom fan-in 2 circuits of size poly(n)(9/5)^n" "matching the lower bound of Goos, Guan, and Mosnoi (Inform. Comput.'24)".
Status: verified (fetched).

Claim H4 (2025). Riazanov, Sofronova, Sokolov: top-down lower bounds for a subclass of AC0[2] containing DNF-of-parities and depth-3 AC0.
Source: ECCC TR25-024. https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2025/024/
Quote: "We give lower bounds for the middle slice function, inner product function, and affine dispersers."
Status: verified (fetched).

SUMMARY TABLE OF "BEST KNOWN" AS OF 2026-10-08
General B_2 circuits, explicit function: 3.1n - o(n) (Li-Yang), verified.
De Morgan formulas: n^3/(log^2 n log log n) (Tal 2014), verified; KRW open, verified.
AC0: exp(Omega(n^{1/(d-1)})) for parity (Hastad 1986); AC0[p]: MOD_m hard unless m power of p (Razborov, Smolensky 1987), secondary; AC0[6] vs NP open, inference.
ACC0: NQP not in ACC0 o THR of size n^{log^k n} (Murray-Williams 2018), verified; a.e. for E^NP (CLW 2020), verified; strong average-case for NQP (Chen-Ren 2020), verified.
TC0: depth-2 LTF gates about n^{3/2}/log^3 n, wires about n^{5/2}/log^{7/2} n (Kane-Williams 2016), verified; no super-polynomial TC0 bound, verified via Chen-Tell.
Monotone: exp(n^{1/2-o(1)}) explicit (CKR 2022), verified; matching 2^{n^{Omega(1)}} (2025), verified; 2^{alpha N} for monotone formulas/span programs/switching networks/comparator circuits (Pitassi-Robere 2017), verified.
Near-maximum 2^n/n: S_2E/1 (CHR 2024), S_2E a.e. (Li 2024), E^{prMA}/1 (Ren-Williams 2026), all verified.
Depth 3: 2^{Theta(sqrt n)} for parity (PPZ), unverified snippet; (9/5)^n for IP with bottom fan-in 2 (2024/2026), verified.
