1. Mathlib: official doc pages for TuringMachine and TMComputable returned 404; the Computability folder has been reorganized into a TuringMachine subdirectory. Machine-checked lower bounds found: PARITY not in AC0 via the switching lemma (Lean, Sept 2026 preprint), bit-PHP in resolution over parities (Lean, Sept 2026 preprint), Clique vs monotone circuits following Gordeev (Isabelle AFP 2022), Kolmogorov incompressibility (Coq ITP 2022, HOL4 CPP 2021, Lean 2026 package), log-rank communication lower bound (Lean, 2026 preprint, snippet only).

5. No SAT-certified circuit lower bound with an independently checked UNSAT certificate was found; Knuth's exhaustive 5-input table (max 12 gates, one NPN class) remains the reference exact small-case result and is itself not certificate-backed.
6. The complexitylib README claims (Cook-Levin, time hierarchy, P/poly, PCP theorem) are self-reports and were not audited; the descriptive-complexity library (arXiv 2609.18261) and a Lean problem page note that Mathlib itself has no complexity classes or resource-bounded reductions.
